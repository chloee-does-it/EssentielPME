FAQ

Frequently asked questions about advertising.

Is the ad budget included in the package?
No. Management fees cover our work. The media budget is paid directly to the platforms by you. Recommended ratio: about 2:1 (media : management).
Which platforms can you advertise on?
All the major platforms: Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Spotify, Reddit and Google Ads. The number of simultaneous platforms depends on your package.
Who provides the ad visuals?
We create the visuals based on your company's brand kit (colors, logo, typography). We also handle ad copywriting in French and English.
When will I see results?
The first data arrives within days, but plan for 4 to 8 weeks of optimization to reach a stable cost per lead.
Can I stop whenever I want?
After the 3-month minimum, yes, with 30 days' notice.

Ready to start your ads?

We set up everything: accounts, pixels and audiences.
