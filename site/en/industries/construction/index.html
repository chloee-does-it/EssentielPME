Is the advertising budget included?

No. Management fees cover our services. The media budget is paid separately to the platforms.

What does campaign management include?

Depending on your package, we handle setup, ad copy, targeting, optimization and reporting. The number of platforms and reporting frequency are agreed on before we start.

Can you target the cities I serve?

Yes. Together, we identify the cities and regions to prioritize based on your services, capacity and the advertising platforms’ options.

What happens during the first call?

We discuss your services, goals, clients and budget. This conversation helps us see whether our service fits your business and explain the next steps.
