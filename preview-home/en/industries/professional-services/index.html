BEFORE WE START

Frequently asked questions

Is the advertising budget included?+

No. Management fees cover our services. The media budget is paid separately to the platforms.

Is this right for my service business?+

Yes. We tailor campaigns to the services and clients of accountants, notaries, lawyers, marketing consultants and other advisors. The first call helps clarify your goals and budget.

What does my package include?+

Depending on your package: setup, ad copy, targeting, optimization and reports. We agree on the number of platforms and reporting frequency before we start.

What happens during the first call?+

We discuss your business, goals, audience and budget. Then we assess whether our service is a good fit and explain the next steps.
